TDSpeed includes the original standard VB-CABLE package by VB-Audio Software.

VB-CABLE is donationware. All participations are welcome. You can identify,
learn about, and donate/pay for VB-CABLE at https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ and
https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm . The original package and its
license are included unchanged in the Drivers\VB-CABLE folder.

TDSpeed reuses an existing usable VB-CABLE or Hi-Fi Cable installation.
Only the standard VB-CABLE package is bundled. Additional paid cable packs
are not bundled. Uninstalling TDSpeed does not uninstall any audio driver.

If no cable is installed, setup requests administrator permission for
VB-Audio's signed driver installer. A Windows restart may be required.

TDSpeed is a personal-use application built with JUCE. JUCE and Microsoft
runtime components retain their respective licenses. The app and installer
are unsigned; the bundled VB-Audio driver package is vendor signed.

Effects in version 0.4.1 embed selected Airwindows DSP (MIT), CloudSeedCore
by Ghost Note Engineering Ltd (MIT), Freeverb3 DSP from Dragonfly Reverb
(GPL version 2 or later, alongside Dragonfly's GPL version 3 notice),
ChowMatrix diffusion (BSD 3-clause), and CHOW Tape's magnetic model (GPLv3).
JUCE modules are available under AGPLv3 or their commercial licence.
Individual licence texts are in the Licences folder. Corresponding adapted
source, JUCE source, tests and build instructions are provided in
Source\TDSpeed-Source-0.4.1.zip. This archive is also included in the portable
package. Third-party authors retain ownership of their respective code.

These are embedded DSP engines with TDSpeed controls. The app does not load
the vendors' plugins or reproduce their full plugin interfaces/preset sets.
The smooth hall is an original Poweramp-inspired listening effect; it is not
an exact Poweramp reverb clone.

RC additionally embeds Signalsmith Basics/DSP (MIT) and MVerb (GPL-3.0-or-later). Full licence texts and pinned revisions are included.
